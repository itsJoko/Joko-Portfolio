# Playground: "Studio island"

A small 3D island floating in fog, where every visitor walks around as a Folio. Walking up to a landmark
opens Joko's real work. Other visitors are there too, with their generated name tags, and everyone can send
the same emoji and preset phrases as on Community. It replaces the design desk on `/playground`.

Decided with Joko on 2026-11-18. Status: step 3 of 7 done (the island is on `/playground` in place of the
desk, as a view with landmark labels, rings and the list; `IslandPiece` / `IslandScene`), plus the art passes. The island is generated by
`art/island/build_island.py` (run in Blender), saved as `art/island/island.blend` and exported to
`public/playground/island.glb`. Look: warm cozy low-poly from Joko's references in `art/island/refs/`
(grass, sand paths, cream plaster, terracotta roof, pond and dock), replacing the grey clay idea below.
Baked shading (ambient occlusion) is still to do.

## Part 1 — How it works

### What it looks like

- **Clay, not textures.** Every surface is a flat colour with soft light, shadow and fog. No painted textures,
  so nothing has to be drawn by hand. Ground and buildings are grey clay. A landmark takes its section hue
  (Projects orange, Graphics sky, Experience violet, Community magenta) when someone walks up to it, the way
  colour is the reward everywhere else on the site.
- **Open-world feel on a small island.** A third-person camera follows your Folio at a slight angle. Fog hides
  the island's edge, and paths, hills, a few trees and rocks make walking feel like exploring. The island is
  small enough to cross in about 20 seconds.
- **Reference images.** Joko can generate concept images (ChatGPT) of the island and each landmark. They go
  in `art/island/refs/` and guide the modelling; they are never shown on the site.

### Landmarks (each opens real content)

| Landmark | Object name | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| Studio (a small workshop with a desk) | `lm_studio` | The selected projects; each opens its sheet on `/projects#slug` |
| Gallery wall (a row of frames) | `lm_gallery` | Graphics collections |
| Notice board | `lm_board` | Experience, the Brief and the CV download |
| Poster stand | `lm_poster` | The live Community poster and a link to `/community` |

Near a landmark, a small card slides up with its name, one line and an **Open** button. The button is the
accessible control: the same landmarks are also listed as chips under the canvas (like the desk's chips), so
keyboard and screen-reader users get everything without walking. The canvas is `aria-hidden`.

### Things to do (decided with Joko, 2026-11-18)

- **Ride a bike.** Walk to the rack and hop on: Folio rides the paths faster (paths and plaza only; bikes
  can't go on grass near water, the dock or into buildings), and parks it back at the rack. Walking stays the
  default. Bikes are a shared world object only for the rider; others see you riding.
- **Knock things over.** A stack of crates, barrels and pots by the café and a few by the commons tumble when
  Folio bumps into them (light physics, a few bodies only), then settle back after a while.
- **Sit on benches.** Walk to a bench and Folio sits; the camera eases out to that bench's view (the lookout
  frames the lake).
- **Light the campfire.** Walk up and the fire grows with smoke and sparks.
- **Hidden collectibles.** A handful of small items (paper planes) hidden around the island; each shows a
  fact about Joko or a project. A counter in the corner and a small reward for finding all of them, saved
  in the visitor's browser. Facts are real ones from PRODUCT.md and the CV only.
- **Day and night** follows the site theme: dark mode is evening, with lamps, lit windows and the fire glowing.
- **Living details:** grass and trees sway, butterflies, ripples and bobbing lily pads, chimney smoke.
- **Sound:** soft ambience (birds, water, footsteps, fire), off until the visitor turns it on.
- **Photo spot:** a frame at the plaza; stand in it to take a picture of your Folio on the island to download.

Their art is modelled (2026-11-18): knock-over stacks by the café's west wall and by the commons, each piece its
own object (`phys_crate_*`, `phys_barrel_*`, `phys_pot_*`; not fixed obstacles in the walk grid); six paper
planes (`collect_plane_01`–`06`: behind the café, beside the garden, behind the gallery, by the campfire, near
the north-east rim, on the dock platform); and the photo frame by the plaza (`block_photo_frame`, walk-through,
with `door_photo` where Folio stands). The build checks every door and plane is reachable from the spawn.

### Walk rules

`public/playground/island-nav.json` (written by `build_island.py`) is a 0.5 m grid over the island in the
site's coordinates (x, and z = -Blender y). A cell is walkable only if all of these hold:

1. It is on the island, inside 92% of the rim: nobody walks off the edge.
2. It is not water, unless it is on the dock or the lookout deck: nobody walks in the lake.
3. It is outside every obstacle: buildings, benches, the garden, props and the planter ring by their real
   (rotated) footprint, fences by their posts and rails, trees and lamps by their trunk. The welcome arch only
   blocks at its posts, so you walk under it.
4. Doors and the spawn are always walkable.

Clicks on blocked cells walk to the nearest walkable cell; landmarks are entered through their `door_*` cell.
The build checks that every door can be reached from the spawn (a flood fill over the grid).

### Moving

- **Click or tap the ground** and your Folio walks there along a short path around obstacles. Clicking a
  landmark walks to its front. Arrow keys and WASD also work on desktop.
- A walk is one message to everyone else (from, to, start time); each browser animates it locally. That keeps
  multiplayer smooth and cheap (see Cost).

### Other visitors

- Everyone on the island is a Folio with a generated name tag (the Community name generator; Shuffle works
  the same way). Up to 20 others are drawn; past that, a "+N here" count.
- **Talk:** the same six emoji and six preset phrases as Community (`src/content/community-words.json`),
  floating up over the sender's Folio. No free text.

### Phones, reduced motion, weak devices

- Phones: tap to walk, the camera a little further out, fewer trees and shadows off.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: a static poster of the island with the landmark chips under it (CLAUDE.md's
  Playground rule). No walking.
- No WebGL, or the scene can't hold about 30 fps: the same poster and chips.

### Architecture

```
Blender (art/island/island.blend) ──export──► public/playground/island.glb (meshopt, about 1 MB)

Browser (/playground, IslandPiece → IslandScene, dynamic import, no SSR)
 ├─ loads island.glb, reads objects by name: walk_ground / walk_path (paths and plaza) / walk_dock (walkable),
 │    water_pond (not walkable), deco_* (decoration, ignored by pathing),
 │    island_base (rock underneath), block_tree_* / block_rock_* and lm_* (obstacles),
 │    door_* (where a Folio stands to use each landmark), spawn (where visitors appear)
 ├─ click → raycast on walk_* → path on a small grid (A*) around block_* → Folio walks
 ├─ near lm_* → landmark card → opens the real page or sheet
 └─ joins channel "island:main" (private) ───────────────► Supabase Realtime
       presence: name + current walk (so newcomers see everyone where they are)
       broadcast: "walk" {from, to, t}, "talk" {reaction or phrase}
```

- **Reuses Community's setup.** Anonymous sign-in with Turnstile, the generated names and the talk lists.
  Sign-in moves into a shared hook so both pages use one session.
- **One migration:** the Realtime policy gets the topic `island:main` next to `community:studio`. No new
  tables: movement never touches the database.
- **Messages are untrusted.** A walk is accepted only if both points are numbers inside the island's bounds
  and its start time is recent; talk only if it is a known emoji or phrase; names only if generated. Anything
  else is dropped (the same rule as Community).
- **Materials by name.** Blender materials are named `clay_light`, `clay_mid`, `clay_dark`, `wood`,
  `leaf`, `hue_projects`, `hue_graphics`, `hue_experience`, `hue_community`. The page swaps them for colours
  read from the site's tokens, so light and dark themes and the section hues stay in one place
  (`globals.css`).
- **Folio as the avatar.** The corner mascot is already built in code (`FolioScene`); the island reuses its
  model with a walk cycle and its face.

### Cost

Free tiers still cover it. Supabase Realtime Free allows 200 concurrent connections (shared with Community)
and 2 million messages a month. Because a walk is one message per click, not a stream of positions, ten
people clicking every three seconds is about 30 messages a second, roughly 18 hours of that crowd a month.
Clicks are throttled to one every 0.4 s. If the island gets busy, the fix is upgrading Supabase, not a
redesign.

### Performance budget

- `island.glb` around 1 MB compressed; under 40k triangles on desktop, a lighter set of trees on phones.
- 60 fps on a mid laptop, 30+ on a mid phone. Renders only while visible (like the desk and the hero arrow).
- Three.js stays inside the Playground and the corner Folio; nothing new loads on other pages.

## Part 2 — Blender (Joko)

### Connecting Claude to Blender

The Blender tools are already in Claude's session (the "Blender" server in the desktop app). They talk to
Blender through an add-on that listens on `localhost:9876`. On 2026-11-18 Blender 5.2.3 (Microsoft Store
build) had no MCP add-on installed, so:

1. Open Blender.
2. **Edit → Preferences → Get Extensions**, search **MCP**, install the Blender MCP add-on. If it isn't
   listed there, the add-on comes from the Blender connector's page in the Claude desktop app's settings.
3. Enable it under **Add-ons**, then start its server (usually from the add-on's panel in the 3D viewport's
   sidebar, opened with **N**).
4. Tell Claude. Claude checks the connection and opens `art/island/island.blend`.

The tools can also run Blender in the background without the window, but that needs the `BLENDER_PATH`
setting in the connector, and the Store build hides its real path, so the add-on route is simpler.

### Editing what Claude makes

The island is generated by `art/island/build_island.py`; running it again rebuilds the whole scene. Moves,
rotations and scaling you make in Blender are kept: run `art/island/capture_overrides.py` (or ask Claude)
and they are saved to `art/island/overrides.json`, which every rebuild applies. Shape edits (Tab mode) are
not kept; ask Claude to put those in the script. The beginner's guide to Blender is `docs/blender-guide.md`.
You can change anything; Claude re-reads the file before each step.

- **Open:** File → Open → `art/island/island.blend`. Save with **Ctrl S**.
- **Look around:** middle mouse to orbit, **Shift** + middle mouse to pan, scroll to zoom, numpad **.** to
  frame the selected object.
- **Move, rotate, scale:** select with a left click, then **G**, **R** or **S**; type **X**, **Y** or **Z** to
  lock to an axis; click to confirm, right click to cancel. **Ctrl Z** undoes.
- **Change a shape:** **Tab** into Edit Mode for vertices, edges and faces; **Tab** back out.
- **Colours:** select an object → Properties panel → Material tab → Base Color. (On the site the named
  materials are replaced by the token colours; change the token in `globals.css` to change it everywhere.)
- **Rules that keep the site working:**
  - Keep object and material names (`lm_studio`, `walk_*`, `clay_light`...). The page finds things by name.
    New props can have any name.
  - No image textures.
  - Keep the island centred on the origin and about 40 m across; Y is up on the site, Z is up in Blender
    (the exporter converts).
- **Export:** run `art/island/export_island.py` (Scripting tab, or ask Claude). It writes
  `public/playground/island.glb` with meshopt compression (about 1.3 MB; the site loads it with
  `useGLTF(url, false, true)`), re-renders `public/playground/island-poster.webp` and saves the `.blend`.
  `build_island.py` also rewrites `public/playground/island-nav.json` (the walk grid) on every run.
